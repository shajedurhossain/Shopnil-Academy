Pacing

How long will this take?

Hours, not weeks

Every module is measured in estimated study hours rather than calendar weeks. A student with 15 free hours a week and someone studying around a full-time job follow the exact same curriculum — they just arrive at different times. Neither is behind.

The core curriculum is about 350 hours. Adding the single-cell specialisation brings it to roughly 460 hours.

Scope

Every organism, not just one

Bioinformatics is not one field. Examples rotate across domains so the skills transfer wherever your research takes you — clinical human genomics, soil metagenomics, or crop breeding.

DomainWhere it appears
Human genomicsVariant calling, GWAS, population genetics, clinical annotation
Microbial & metagenomicsAssembly, taxonomic classification, functional annotation
Model organismsDevelopmental biology, functional genomics, gene knockouts
Plants & cropsGenomic selection, polyploidy, breeding populations, stress response
Cross-domainQuality control, alignment, file formats, workflow automation, containers

Plant and crop genomics gets unusually deep coverage here. Most bioinformatics courses online are human-heavy, and plant researchers are badly underserved — so where a technique genuinely lives in plant science, such as genomic selection, that is how it is taught.
