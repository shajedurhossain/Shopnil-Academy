Chapter 1 · Lesson 1
Гласные и мягкость
Vowels & Soft Pairs
📖 Vocabulary
💡
Key idea
Russian vowels come in hard/soft pairs. A soft vowel signals that the consonant right before it is "softened" (palatalized) — pronounced with a faint y-glide blended in. You'll hear this constantly, so learning the pairs now — before you meet any consonants — pays off in every lesson that follows.
Hard Vowels Твёрдые гласные
LetterSounds like
1 а"ah" — father
2 о"oh" — more
3 у"oo" — moon
4 ыno English match — say "ee" while pulling your tongue back
5 э"eh" — bet
Soft Vowels Мягкие гласные
LetterSounds like
1 я"yah" — yard
2 ё"yo" — yolk (always stressed)
3 ю"yoo" — you
4 и"ee" — see
5 е"yeh" — yes
🧠 Pairing trick
Memorize the hard and soft vowels in matching order — it's the exact order Russians recite them in, and it will make noun-ending and verb-ending patterns predictable for the rest of the course.
а → я о → ё у → ю ы → и э → е

🎤 Pronunciation
🎤
Pronunciation tips
Hold each vowel clearly and a touch longer than in English — Russian vowels are crisp. For ы, keep your tongue pulled back and lips relaxed (not rounded); it's the sound most English speakers find hardest at first.

📚 Grammar
The Hard/Soft System Твёрдость и мягкость
Hard vowelSoft vowelWhat the soft vowel signals
аяConsonant before it is palatalized ("softened")
оёSame — plus ё is always the stressed syllable
уюSame softening effect
ыиSame softening effect
эеSame softening effect
💡 Why this matters
You haven't met any consonants yet, but this rule is the single biggest reason Russian spelling looks the way it does. Once you reach Lesson 2, every consonant you learn will change its sound slightly depending on whether it's followed by a hard or a soft vowel. Knowing the five pairs by heart now means that rule will feel automatic later — you won't be learning two things at once.
One exception to remember Исключение
RuleNote
ё is always stressedUnlike every other vowel, ё never appears in an unstressed syllable. If you see ё, that's where the stress falls.
ы never starts a wordы only appears after a consonant, never at the beginning of a native Russian word.

📖 Reading
Russian has capital and lowercase forms, just like English — but a few capitals look different from their lowercase partner. Practice reading them here before you meet consonants.
Capital & Lowercase Заглавные и строчные
А аSame shape, just bigger — easy
О оSame shape, just bigger — easy
У уCapital keeps the descender — watch the tail
Ы ыNever capitalized at the start of a word (see Grammar tab)
Э эSame shape, just bigger
Я яSame shape, just bigger
Ё ёSame shape — the two dots are often dropped in adult writing, but keep them while you're learning
Ю юSame shape, just bigger
И иSame shape, just bigger
Е еSame shape, just bigger — don't confuse with Latin "E"
👀 Reading check
А О У Ы Э   ·   Я Ё Ю И Е
1. Which of these is the capital form of "ю"?
2. Which vowel is never written as a capital at the start of a word?
